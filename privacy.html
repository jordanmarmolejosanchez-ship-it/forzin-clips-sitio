Privacy policy

BEIK STUDIO · Last updated: November 7, 2026

BEIK STUDIO is a Windows desktop application that records the screen, organizes recordings in a library and publishes them to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It is a private tool created and used by Jordan Marmolejo Sanchez. This policy explains what data the application touches and what it does with it.

Who is responsible

Jordan Marmolejo Sanchez. Contact: Jordanmarmolejosanchez@gmail.com.

What data the application handles

  • Your recordings and captures. Videos, thumbnails, copies with text and reports are stored on your own computer, in the Videos\BEIK STUDIO folder and in the application’s data folder.
  • The library. A local database with each clip’s name, date, duration and status, and the publishing history.
  • The credentials for your accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok), once you connect them. They are stored on your computer using Windows’ protection mechanisms. You connect each account once and can disconnect it at any time.
  • The minimum account details each platform returns when you connect, such as the channel, Page or account name, so the application can show which account you are about to publish to.

What the application does NOT do

  • It does not send your recordings, credentials or data to any server owned by the author or to third parties, except the publication you request.
  • It uses no analytics, trackers, advertising or profiling.
  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It never publishes anything without your explicit authorization for each publication.

When anything leaves your computer

Only when you press Publish. At that moment the application sends the video, title, description and hashtags you wrote to the platform or platforms you chose, using the account you connected.

Third-party services

Each platform handles data under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • YouTube. The YouTube Data API is used to upload videos to your channel. By connecting your account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. BEIK STUDIO’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta). The Meta API is used to publish Reels to your Facebook Page and your professional Instagram account.
  • TikTok. TikTok’s Content Posting API is used to send videos to your account.

How to withdraw access and delete your data

  • You can disconnect an account in the application, or revoke access from each platform’s security settings (Google, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok).
  • To delete local data, remove the Videos\BEIK STUDIO folder and the application’s data folder, or uninstall the application.
  • If you want any data about you that the author might hold to be deleted, write to Jordanmarmolejosanchez@gmail.com.

Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the application adds features that change how data is handled, for example artificial intelligence services that analyze your clips, this policy will be updated first and your consent will be requested. The date above shows the latest revision.
