Privacy policy

FORZIN CLIPS · Last updated: November 8, 2026

FORZIN CLIPS is a Windows desktop app that records your screen and other sources, organizes recordings in a library, and lets you publish them to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. This policy explains what data the app touches and what it does with it. The app was previously called BEIK STUDIO.

Who is responsible

Jordan Marmolejo Sanchez, author of the app. Contact: Jordanmarmolejosanchez@gmail.com.

Data the app handles

  • Recordings and captures: videos, thumbnails, text copies and diagnostic reports. They are stored on your computer, in the Videos\FORZIN CLIPS folder and the app's data folder. The sources you capture (screen, window, camera, microphone or system audio) are processed only on your computer.
  • Library: a local database with each clip's name, date, duration and status, plus your publishing history.
  • Access tokens: when you connect a YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok account, the app stores on your computer the access tokens the platform issues, encrypted with Windows protection. The app never sees or stores your passwords. You connect each account once and can disconnect it at any time.
  • Basic account details: the channel, Page or account name each platform returns, so you can see where you are about to publish.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send recordings, tokens or other data to the author or to any server of its own. FORZIN CLIPS has no servers.
  • It uses no analytics, trackers, advertising or profiling.
  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It publishes nothing without your explicit authorization for each publication.

When data leaves your computer

Only when you press "Publish". The app then sends the video, title, description and hashtags to the platform you chose, through the account you connected.

Third-party services

  • YouTube / Google: FORZIN CLIPS uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your channel. By connecting your account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. FORZIN CLIPS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): the app uses Meta's APIs to publish Reels to a Facebook Page and a professional Instagram account. The Facebook Terms, the Instagram Terms and the Meta Privacy Policy apply.
  • TikTok: the app uses TikTok's Content Posting API to send videos to your account. TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply.

Each platform handles data under its own terms and policies.

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Revoke access: disconnect the account inside the app, which deletes the stored tokens from your computer. You can also revoke access in the security settings of Google, Facebook (Business Integrations), Instagram (Apps and Websites) or TikTok (Security and permissions).
  • Delete local data: remove the Videos\FORZIN CLIPS folder and the app's data folder, or uninstall the app. If you used an earlier version, a Videos\BEIK STUDIO folder may also exist.
  • Request deletion: FORZIN CLIPS keeps no data about you outside your computer. If you still want to request deletion of anything the author might hold, email Jordanmarmolejosanchez@gmail.com.

Retention

Local data stays on your computer until you delete it. Access tokens are deleted when you disconnect the account. Data already on YouTube, Meta or TikTok is governed by each platform's policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

Today FORZIN CLIPS does not send your clips to any artificial intelligence service. If it later adds features that do, for example to generate titles or descriptions, I will update this policy before turning them on and the app will ask for your consent. The date above shows the latest revision.
