How it works

From your stream to your socials in 3 steps

No complicated editors, no uploading files by hand.

1

Record or hit FORZIN

Record your screen, a window, your camera or a Kick, YouTube or Twitch stream. Something epic happened? Hit FORZIN (on your PC, phone or Stream Dock) and save what happened and what comes next.

2

Shape the clip

Switch to vertical 9:16, move and scale the video, fill the gaps with a blurred background, add styled captions and let tracking frame the person.

3

Publish in one click

Pick your networks and hit Publish: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels and Instagram Reels. If your connection drops, the upload resumes on its own.

Features

Everything you need for your clips

Real screenshots of the app (interface in Spanish).

Studio

Your recording canvas, with scenes and sources like your favorite streaming software.

  • Screen, window, camera and images on one canvas
  • Microphone and system audio with floating controls
  • FORZIN button: saves the last minutes even if you weren't recording
  • Landscape, vertical and square formats

FORZIN button

Saves the moment even if you weren't recording: what happened and the reaction that follows.

  • Before only, before and after, or custom
  • The ring turns orange while it saves what comes next
  • Press again to extend the clip, with no repeated scenes
  • Also with Ctrl+Shift+F, the Stream Dock or your phone

FORZIN Voice

Pro filters for your mic in one click, in real time.

  • Styles: Natural, Podcast, Streamer, Radio, Noisy room
  • AI noise removal and noise gate
  • EQ and compressor for an even voice
  • Save your own presets

FORZIN Reverb

Give your voice the sound of a room, with a 3D view of the space.

  • Ready-made styles and filter, space, tail and mix knobs
  • Effect amount in one control
  • Undo everything with Ctrl+Z

Split and tracking

Two people in one vertical clip: one frame on top and one below, each following its person.

  • 1 person or 2 people
  • Each frame Fixed or "Follow" (tracks a face)
  • Draw the frames by dragging on the canvas
  • The recording stays complete

FORZIN Mobile

Control FORZIN from your phone, on any network.

  • Alerts when a Kick, YouTube or Twitch channel goes live
  • Watch the stream, hit FORZIN and turn on automatic clips
  • Approve, title and publish each clip
  • Secure connection to your PC, no open ports

Stream Dock

Every feature on a key, with its state in sight.

  • FORZIN, record, pause, scenes and mic
  • Stream sound, PC audio and automatic clips
  • Go live, switch streams and publish the last clip

Vertical clips

Turn any recording into a Short or a Reel without leaving the app.

  • 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 and 4:5 formats
  • Move and scale the video right on the player
  • Black or blurred-video background
  • Styled captions: TikTok, neon, box, highlighted word…

Crop

Crop and straighten any source or video precisely.

  • Free, 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9 and 9:16 ratios
  • Rotation that snaps to 0°
  • Undo everything with Ctrl+Z

Auto tracking

FORZIN detects the person and moves the frame like a pro camera operator.

  • Face, upper body or full body
  • Smooth moves, no jumps or jitter
  • Analyzed on your PC, your video is never sent anywhere

FAQ

Common questions

When does FORZIN CLIPS launch?

Launch is planned for January 2027. Hit "Notify me" and we'll let you know when it's ready.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Do I need video editing skills?

No. Pick the format, move the video with your mouse, type your caption and hit Publish.

Are my videos uploaded to a FORZIN server?

No. FORZIN CLIPS has no servers. Your clips only leave your PC when you publish them, straight to the network you choose.

Can I clip another channel's live stream?

Yes, from Kick, YouTube and Twitch, by adding the channel in FORZIN Mobile. Only do it with creators who allow you to clip their content.

What is FORZIN Mobile?

A phone app that connects securely to your PC (without opening router ports): it alerts you when a channel goes live, and from there you hit FORZIN, turn on automatic clips and approve or publish.

Why does it arrive as a draft on TikTok?

FORZIN sends the clip to your TikTok inbox. There you write the final caption and post it from the TikTok app.

Can I disconnect my accounts?

Yes, anytime, from Settings. The access stored on your PC is deleted.
