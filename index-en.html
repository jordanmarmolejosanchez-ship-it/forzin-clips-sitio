FORZIN CLIPS

Record your screen, organize your clips and publish them to YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels and TikTok from a single Windows app.

Record several sources

Screen, window, camera, microphone and system audio, combined on one vertical canvas.

Local library

Every recording is registered on your computer, with its thumbnail, status and history.

Publish with your permission

You connect your accounts once and every publication needs your explicit authorization.

Your data stays with you

No servers of its own, no analytics or ads. Tokens are stored encrypted with Windows.
